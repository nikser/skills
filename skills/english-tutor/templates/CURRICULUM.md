# Curriculum — 12 weeks, A2 → B1+ (work / IT English)

Example curriculum for an A2 learner who needs English for work in tech. Re-sequence it after the
placement test: a failed axis moves earlier, untaught material stays where it is.

Each week: grammar axis (slot A), vocabulary/context axis (slot B), production genre (slot C).
Day 7 of every week is a **review day**: only the week's errors + SRS, no new topics.

## Stage 1 — close A2 (weeks 1–4), difficulty 1.0–1.2

| Week | A: grammar | B: vocabulary / context | C: production |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Present perfect for experience: ever / never / already / yet | I've worked with Kafka, I haven't tested it yet | "my experience with a technology", 3 sentences; reply to "any updates?" |
| 2 | Present simple vs continuous; frequency adverbs; question word order | role, stack, daily tasks; I work on / I'm working on | daily standup: yesterday / today / blockers |
| 3 | Past simple: irregular verbs, questions and negatives (did / didn't) | last week: incident, fix, release | mini-report on last week |
| 4 | Future: going to / will / present continuous for plans; time clauses | planning and estimates: estimate, deadline, by Friday | "plan for the week"; "when will it be ready" + review day |

## Stage 2 — core B1 (weeks 5–8), difficulty 1.2–1.5

| Week | A: grammar | B: vocabulary / context | C: production |
|---|---|---|---|
| 5 | Present perfect vs past simple (just / already / yet vs yesterday / ago) | I fixed it yesterday / I've already fixed it | status update for a manager |
| 6 | Articles a / an / the / zero; countable / uncountable; in / on / at | a bug, some logs, much traffic, on Monday, in the sprint | ticket description, 3–4 sentences |
| 7 | 1st and 2nd conditional; modals of probability (might / must be / can't be) | risks and suggestions: if we deploy on Friday, I'd suggest | 3 PR comments: praise, question, blocking remark |
| 8 | Passive (present / past simple); past continuous | incidents: was caused by, has been rolled back | short incident update + review day, stage test |

## Stage 3 — B1+ → B2 entry (weeks 9–12), difficulty 1.5–1.8

| Week | A: grammar | B: vocabulary / context | C: production |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9 | Reported speech (basic backshift); relative clauses who / which / that | he said he would, the service which handles auth | meeting recap in 3 sentences; call summary email |
| 10 | Present perfect continuous vs simple; for / since | I've been debugging, we've been migrating since Monday | "what I did for 3 days and why it took long" |
| 11 | Gerund vs infinitive (stop / remember / avoid / manage to); phrasal verbs | roll out, look into, come up with, run into, break down | 5 sentences with phrasal verbs from own work |
| 12 | Modal perfects (should have done); diplomatic disagreement; discourse markers | I see your point, but / to be fair / however, as a result | arguing a technical decision with hedging + final test |

## Placement test (day 1, all three slots)

- Slot A: 8 grammar questions A1 → B2 in rising order — finds the ceiling.
- Slot B: 6 vocabulary / collocation questions.
- Slot C: 3–4 free sentences about the learner's work: tenses, articles, word order, range.
- Result → `state.json` `verified_level` + `difficulty`; weak spots → `errors.json`.

## Honest time budget

CEFR guidance: A2 → B1 is 180–250 guided learning hours. Three 5-minute sessions a day is about
1.7 h/week. That keeps grammar accurate and grows active vocabulary, but drills alone do not reach B1 —
say so to the learner and recommend extra input (reading / listening at A2–B1) and speaking.
